# A little credit for your next fax

Fax It now offers a way to earn free fax credits: invite a friend who signs in and claims your invitation. You can still buy credits whenever you need to send, without a sending subscription.

## An invitation, not a purchase requirement

Open referrals in Fax It and share your invitation. Your friend signs in and claims it in the app. Sharing the link alone does not earn credits, and neither of you needs to make a purchase or send a fax to accept the offer.

The app shows the reward and eligibility before the claim. Each account can accept one incoming invitation and cannot claim its own code. Reward amounts can vary by offer and participant. A pending grant is still being processed; confirmed credits appear in the account balance.

## The same balance across your Apple devices

Referral credits join the same balance as purchased credits. Use that balance on iPhone, iPad, or Mac with the same signed-in account. Unused credits do not expire.

One credit sends one page to a US or Canadian destination. Other supported destinations use two credits per page, and a cover page counts. Free credits are not a promise of unlimited free faxing or free receiving.

## Keep the choice

For an occasional fax, buy credits or use credits you have earned. If you need your own receiving number, optional ad-free monthly plans provide an available US or Canadian number and the usage shown in the app. Receiving still requires an active number subscription.

See the current options on the [Fax It product page](https://37.technology/faxit), or [download Fax It for iPhone, iPad, and Mac](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fax-it-send-receive-fax/id1458261691).
