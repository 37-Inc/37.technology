# Fax It Comes to Mac

Fax It is now available as a native Mac app. The release brings the same direct fax workflow to a larger screen: assemble the document, check the pages, send it, and follow the result.

## A Mac-shaped fax workflow

The Mac app was built around the way documents already move on macOS. You can import PDFs and images, drag files into a new fax, remove or reorder pages, and review the document before it leaves your computer.

Once a fax is sent, its status and history remain available in the app.

## Pay per page is still here

Fax It for Mac keeps the existing credit model for sending. Buy the pages you need, use them when you need them, and keep the remaining credits for a later fax. Sending does not require a monthly subscription.

That matters because many faxes are occasional but important: a signed form, a medical document, a government application, or paperwork for a recipient that still requires fax.

## Available from the App Store

Fax It is available for Mac, iPhone, and iPad through the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fax-it-send-fax-from-phone/id1458261691). Learn more about the sending workflow on the [Fax It product page](/faxit).
