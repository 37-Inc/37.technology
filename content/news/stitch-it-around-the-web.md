# Stitch It Around the Web

Independent writers have been explaining Stitch It in their own languages and contexts since shortly after its 2012 launch. Together, those articles form a useful record of how people have used screenshot stitching over time.

The pages below describe the app as it existed when each piece was published. They are historical references, not current feature or pricing documentation, and inclusion here does not imply an endorsement. For current details, use the [Stitch It product page](/stitch-it), the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/stitch-it-long-screenshots/id554594252), or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.luckybunnyllc.stitchit).

## 2012: An early Italian introduction

In December 2012, the Italian publication [Focus](https://www.focus.it/tecnologia/tecno-prodotti/condividi-con-gli-amici-le-tue-chat-piu-divertenti) described Stitch It as a way to combine a long conversation from multiple screenshots. The piece also noted the option to obscure private details before sharing.

That article appeared less than two months after the original App Store release and captures the first version of the problem Stitch It set out to solve.

## 2014: A German walkthrough

[appgefahren](https://www.appgefahren.de/stich-it-screenshots-von-nachrichten-108058.html) published a German walkthrough in June 2014. It focused on the hands-on process of selecting screenshots, sorting them, cropping repeated areas, and joining them into one image.

The description is still recognizable today: the person making the image controls the order and the seam rather than relying on an automatic result.

## 2015 and 2016: Discovery and Android

Stitch It received a [Product Hunt listing](https://www.producthunt.com/products/stitch-it) in 2015, bringing the app to another product-discovery community.

In August 2016, Italy's [SmartWorld](https://www.smartworld.it/app/stitch-it-app-screenshot-416456.html) covered the Android release. The article documents Stitch It crossing from its original iOS home to a second mobile platform. Like any contemporaneous review, its observations reflect that version of the app.

## 2021: A Japanese tutorial

The Japanese site [BamKa](https://bamka.info/screenshot-merge-stitchit) published a detailed tutorial in January 2021. It demonstrated the practical workflow for making a long screenshot and showed that the original use case continued to be relevant years after launch.

## Community recommendations

Stitch It has also surfaced in community answers to practical questions. An [iPhoneHelp discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/iphonehelp/comments/bs6dbc/how_to_take_scrolling_screenshot_in_iphone/) recommended it in 2019 to someone looking for a scrolling-screenshot workflow. A [2024 r/iOS discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/ios/comments/1db938w/share_your_lesser_known_or_niche_ios_apps/) included it among lesser-known or niche apps.

These references are small, independent signals rather than formal reviews. They matter because they show the same job recurring across years, countries, and communities: someone has more than one screen of information and wants to share it as one coherent image.
