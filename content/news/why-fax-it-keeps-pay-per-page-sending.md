# Why Fax It Keeps Pay-Per-Page Sending

A monthly plan can make sense when faxing is part of the weekly routine. It makes less sense when the job is one form that needs to reach a doctor, agency, attorney, or office today.

Fax It was built for that second situation.

## Buy pages, not a commitment

Sending with Fax It uses page credits. You choose a document, review the pages and destination, and spend credits when the fax is sent. Unused credits do not expire at the end of the month.

There is no subscription required for the basic sending workflow. If another fax appears six months later, the remaining balance is still there.

## Why the distinction matters

The cost of occasional software should match the occasional job. A person sending a government form once should not have to remember to cancel a recurring plan afterward.

That principle also keeps the product easier to understand:

- Page credits pay for sent pages.
- The app shows the document before sending.
- Delivery status stays with the fax history.
- Remaining credits stay available for next time.

## A model we intend to keep

Fax It can grow without removing the simpler option. Pay-per-page sending remains available for people who only need to get a fax out the door.
