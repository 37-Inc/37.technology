# Stitch It Comes to Mac

Stitch It is now available as a native Mac app. The release brings its arrange, crop, redact, and export workflow to screenshots and other images on your desktop, with a live preview of the complete result.

A conversation, a receipt, a sequence of instructions, or a side-by-side comparison can all start as separate images. Stitch It helps you turn them into one clear result.

## See the whole result while you work

Import PNG or JPEG files, choose images from Photos, or paste from the clipboard. Put them in order and choose a vertical or horizontal layout. Trim overlaps with crop handles or enter exact pixel amounts.

The live preview shows the complete stitch as you edit. You can check how the images fit together without leaving the workspace. It is a manual workflow: you decide the order, the crop, and where each image meets the next.

The Mac app is built with SwiftUI and AppKit. It supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 or later.

## Hide details, then export

Cover names, addresses, account details, or other sensitive information with solid-color rectangles or a freehand brush. Choose the color and brush width, and use undo and redo while you work.

When the result is ready, export a JPEG, PNG, or PDF to a file on your Mac.

## Keep an editable project on your Mac

Save a Stitch It project and reopen it later with its images, order, trims, and marks intact. You can return to the work without starting from scratch.

Editable projects retain the original images. If you are hiding private details, share the exported image or PDF, not the project file. Mac projects do not currently open for editing in the iPhone or iPad app.

## Start free, with no Mac ads

Stitch It for Mac is free to download and supports up to three images per project, with a small watermark on exports. Pro lifts the image limit and removes the watermark. There are no ads on Mac, whether you use the free version or Pro.

Apple Pro purchases can be restored across iPhone, iPad, and Mac using the same Apple Account. No separate Stitch It login is required. Android remains a separate purchase platform.
