# What We Mean by an Independent Software Company

Thirty Seven builds software and operates its own portfolio. That is the simplest description of the company, but it also shapes how we work.

## The products come first

The portfolio is not a gallery of old client projects. It is a set of products we continue to design, build, release, support, and maintain.

Today that includes software for rehabilitation, screenshot stitching, faxing, video reframing, color extraction, elevation, and gift discovery. Each product keeps its own visual identity and audience. Thirty Seven provides the operating home behind them.

You can [explore the portfolio](/#work).

## Operating changes the work

Owning the result means staying for the parts after launch: support requests, store reviews, platform changes, reliability work, pricing decisions, and the next release.

That feedback reaches the product and engineering work directly. There is no point where a finished project is handed off and forgotten.

## Selective client work

Thirty Seven also works with founders and small teams when the fit is right. Our deepest experience is in native iOS, with shipped work across Android, web, and backend systems.

The same operating perspective applies to client work. The goal is dependable software that can ship and remain useful, with product guidance and post-launch support when they matter.
